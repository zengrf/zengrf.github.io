Configurations

Apollonius' problem admits 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 or 8 tangent circles — never 7. Each button loads three circles realising that count.

The 0, 4 and 8 sets fill open regions and hold their count while you drag. The rest sit on a tangency boundary, so they change the moment a circle moves — that is where the odd counts live.
