  <h2>Project overview</h2>
  <figure>
    <img src="{{ '/assets/img/ogura-calligraphy.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Calligraphy study of the Ogura Hyakunin Isshu" />

  <p>I am gradually translating, annotating, and typesetting the poems of the Ogura Hyakunin Isshu (小倉百人一首). Each sheet blends calligraphy practice, historical research, and modern commentary aimed at students encountering classical Japanese literature for the first time.</p>
</section>

<section class="section">
  <h2>Workflow</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Digitize the original 色紙 (shikishi) cards and restore the gold-leaf texture.</li>
    <li>Provide a literal translation, followed by cultural or mathematical footnotes whenever a poem invites a combinatorial interpretation.</li>
    <li>Compile vocabulary lists for students studying classical Japanese (bungo) alongside Mandarin glosses.</li>
  </ul>
</section>
