## Seminars

{% include figure.html image=page.seminar_image %}

### Writing Milestone Seminar

In Spring 2025, I helped organize the [Writing Milestone Seminar](https://sites.math.washington.edu/~zawadx/milestone_sem.html) with [Zawad Chowdhury](https://sites.math.washington.edu/~zawadx/). This seminar gives beginning graduate students working on their [writing milestone](https://math.washington.edu/writing-milestone) a chance to speak about the math topics that they have been working so closely on, and to discuss this new and emerging mutual experience with fellow graduate students.

### Algebraic Curves

In Winter 2025, I organized a graduate reading course on algebraic curves, supervised by [Giovanni Inchiostro](https://math.washington.edu/people/giovanni-inchiostro). More details at [Algebraic Curves Learning Seminar](https://sites.google.com/uw.edu/algebraic-curves-seminar/home).

### MATH 583C — Intersection Theory

In Spring 2024, I co-organized a graduate reading course on intersection theory together with [Arkamouli Debnath](https://sites.google.com/view/arkamouli/courses-and-research?authuser=0), supervised by [Sándor Kovács](https://math.washington.edu/people/sandor-kovacs). Notes and schedule can be found at the course website [MATH 583C Intersection Theory](https://sites.google.com/view/uwmath583cintersectiontheory).

## Mentorship

{% include figure.html image=page.mentorship_image %}

I have partaken in the UW Math AI Lab, the Washington eXperiental Math Lab (WXML), and the Washington Directed Reading Program (WDRP).

### [UW Math AI Lab](https://sites.math.washington.edu/ai/)

I am a mentor on the project [Reinforcement Learning for Polynomials](https://github.com/uw-math-ai/PolyArithmeticCircuitsRL/tree/rohan-new-branch). Together with [Jarod Alper](https://sites.math.washington.edu//~jarod/) and other group members, we try to train a AlphaZero-style reinforcement learning agent to efficiently compute polynomial circuits. This can be viewed as an extremely simplified version of auto-proof generation. This project has produced an ICLR workshop paper titled [CircuitBuilder: From Polynomials to Circuits via Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17075).

I am a mentor on the project [CayleyPy: Advancing Group Theory with AI](https://github.com/uw-math-ai/cayley-py).

I served as a mentor on the project "What mathematical functions can neural networks learn?" in the 2024-2025 iteration of the Math AI Lab.

### [Washington eXperimental Mathematics Lab - WXML](https://wxml.math.washington.edu/)

I am a mentor on the Spring 2026 project *"Calculus Visuals – Interactive modeling with Desmos, 3D prints, and other tools"*.

I served as a mentor on the Spring 2025 project *"Creating 3D Prints and Animations to Help Teach Calculus."* With [Andy Loveless](https://sites.math.washington.edu/~aloveles/) and other group members, we created 3D prints of mathematical objects as teaching aids for the calculus sequence, and geometric thinking in general.
