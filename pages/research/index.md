<h2>Focus Areas</h2>
<p>My research sits at the interface of algebraic geometry, homotopy theory, and combinatorics. I am especially interested in moduli of singular varieties, localization techniques in enumerative geometry, and computational approaches using Sage and Macaulay2.</p>
<ul>
  <li>Intersection theory on moduli spaces and applications to classical projective geometry.</li>
  <li>Equivariant and equivariant-derived enhancements of algebraic K-theory.</li>
  <li>Combinatorial models for Coxeter and Bruhat-type phenomena.</li>
</ul>
</div>
