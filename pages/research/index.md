  <h3> Research Interests </h3>
  I am interested in Schubert calculus, equivariant intersection theory, and moduli theory. Recently, I became invested in computing $K$-theory and Chow rings of varieties and stacks. I am also interested in formalization of mathematics and using machine learning to aid mathematical discoveries.
